# ADR-0007: Mid-day random face challenges are vetoed by the product owner
Status: Accepted (policy ruling 2026-07-08)
Attendance face verification (start/end day) stays. Compensating controls: motion-signature
telemetry, dwell correlation, start/end-day verify. Residual phone-parking/buddy-punching risk
is measured explicitly in the Patna pilot. Reintroduction requires a product-owner RFC.
